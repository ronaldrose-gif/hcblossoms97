About Holy Cross Blossoms 🌸

An integral part of the Institute of Rehabilitation Science, Holy Cross College, Trichy — serving the special children of Trichy since 17 July 1997.

Our Beginning

A blossoming of beautiful buds

The foundation of Blossoms Pre-school was laid on 17 July 1997. The remarkable occasion was graced by Mr. Joseph Xavier,District Rehabilitation Officer, as Chief Guest, who inaugurated the Blossoms Pre-school, and Ms. Sian Tesni, International CBM Consultant.

On the 20th year earmark of the Department of Rehabilitation Science, with help from CBM, Rev. Sr. Celestine, Provincial, Trichy Province inaugurated the new building on 9 July 2003. The Department of PG & Research Rehabilitation Science guides our programmes, and the Department of Audiology and Speech & Language Pathology kindly provides speech therapy and audiogram tests for our children.

“By the Grace of God and in His light, we make our intellectually challenged children blossom, shine and make a difference in the society where they have bloomed.”

👁️ Vision

Looking Beyond Ability

🎯 Aim

To enhance the Quality of Life of Children with Special Needs.

💜 Mission

To encourage the Children with Special Needs to enjoy their rights and privileges at every level in the society.

Our Objectives

What we work for, every day

💪

Confidence & Mainstreaming

To develop self-confidence and prepare children to join the mainstream.

📋

Assess, Train, Develop

To assess, train and develop basic skills in children and provide need-based education.

🏠

Independent Daily Living

To give equal opportunities to children with special needs to be independent in their daily living.

💼

Economic Independence

To prepare them to become economically independent persons.

👨‍👩‍👧

Parent Guidance

To provide guidance and counselling for parents to accept and care for their children with special needs.

📣

Awareness & Prevention

To give more awareness programmes to prevent disability and to sensitize the public on various issues of disability.

⚖️

Rights & Advocacy

To lobby with the government for the rights of people with special needs.

🌐

Networking

To network with NGOs catering to people with special needs at Local / State / National level.

🦋

Dignity for Every Child

To build a society of love and treat everyone as God's gift — looking always beyond ability.

Milestones

Our journey of grace & service

17 JULY 1997

🌱 Blossoms is founded

Blossoms Pre-school inaugurated by Mr. Joseph Xavier, District Rehabilitation Officer, in the presence of Ms. Sian Tesni, International CBM Consultant.

9 JULY 2003

🏫 New building inaugurated

Rev. Sr. Celestine, Provincial, Trichy Province inaugurated the new building with support from CBM, marking 20 years of the Department of Rehabilitation Science.

30 APRIL 2007

📜 Registration & Recognition

School Registration and Recognition received under the PwD Act, 1995.

31 MARCH 2008

👩‍🏫 Salary grant received

Salary grant for 2 Special Educators and 1 Physiotherapist from the State Commissioner for PwD. (Caretaker salary grant added on 21.06.2023.)

15 DECEMBER 2012

🕯️ Vocational floor opened

Second floor extended for vocational training, inaugurated by Mrs. Shamala, DDAWO, Trichy.

1 MARCH 2013

♿ RSRTC EIC — Multiple Disability Project

Project awarded to our school — consolidated pay for 2 special educators & 1 caretaker; noon meals for EIC children from February 2014 with travelling allowance from the State Commissioner for PwD.

2022 – 2025

🔄 Renewal under RPwD Act, 2016

Renewal of Registration and Recognition under the Rights of Persons with Disabilities Act, 2016 — valid 01.08.2022 to 31.07.2025.

10 JUNE 2024

🍱 Government noon meals

The Tamil Nadu Government provides noon meals for Holy Cross Blossoms School for Intellectual Disability, with travelling allowance to bring food from the nearby government school.

TODAY

🌸 Still blossoming

64 children in the school + 24 children in the Multiple Disability Project — cared for by special educators, physiotherapists, speech & occupational therapists and caretakers. Head Mistress: Sr. Josephine.
