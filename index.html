Welcome to Blossoms

A school where beautiful buds blossom 🌺

The name “BLOSSOMS” is a beautiful one — it conjures up the true essence of what a special child should be: a blossoming of beautiful buds. Started on 17 July 1997 for the benefit of the special children of Trichy, our multidisciplinary team assesses, plans intervention and evaluates every child's progress regularly.

  • This year: 64 children cared for by 8 special educators, 2 physiotherapists, 2 speech therapists, 1 occupational therapist & 2 caretakers
  • 25 children in inclusive education in neighbourhood schools
  • Special coaching — 7 students preparing for the 10th public exam
  • Theme of the year: Therapeutic Grounded Education

🧠

Intellectual Disability

🧩

Autism Spectrum Disorder (ASD)

Structured learning, communication, articulation & speech-language therapy and social-skill development.

⚡

Attention-building activities, yoga, movement therapy and positive behaviour support.

Down Syndrome

Early stimulation, speech therapy and joyful concept-based learning at every child's pace.

📖

Learning & Speech Disorders

Slow learning, learning disability, developmental delay and speech & language disorder support — including mainstream exam preparation.

Integrated & Inclusive Education

Our children are integrated into nearby Government-aided schools to build social skills and mingle with peers. We train children with learning disability, mild intellectual disability and autism for 8th & 10th standard government exams — and arrange scribe facility for them.
