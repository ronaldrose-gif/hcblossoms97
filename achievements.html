Achievements & Celebrations 🏆

Our children and staff have excelled at State, National and International levels — because every ability deserves a stage.

Sports

State • National • International

Our school conducts sports events every year, and our children have excelled in competitions at every level.

  • 21 August 2023 — At Holy Cross College's 100th Sports Day (Chief Guest: Thiru Udhayanidhi Stalin, Minister for Youth Welfare & Sports Development; with Thiru K. N. Nehru & Thiru Anbil Mahesh), 40 of our students gave a mind-blowing performance of colourful games and dance.
  • Selvan Devakar, Selvan Aravind Subramanian and Selvan Naveen Akash were honoured for representing as athletes at the 2013 Asia-Pacific Special Olympics, Australia.
  • 18 September 2024 — Master Shri Masi won 1st prize in Silambam & Surulval at the District-level competition conducted by the World Silambam Youth Association.
  • 2–3 August 2024 — Sr. Nancy & Mrs. Anita completed the District-level Special Olympics Bharat coaches training programme.
Awards & Honours

Proud moments for the Blossoms family

🏅

Best Employee Achiever — Govt. of Tamil Nadu

22 January 2024: Ms. Sounderavalli, caretaker of Blossoms Special School, was awarded Best Employee Achiever (Multiple Disability category) by the Government of Tamil Nadu. She received the award from the Honourable Chief Minister Thiru M. K. Stalin at the Tamil Nadu Secretariat, Chennai.

🏆

Talentia Sadhana 2024 — First Place

10 December 2024: At the World Disability Day celebration “Talentia Sadhana 2024” by the Dept. of Rehabilitation Science & SOCSEAD, our school won First Place among 21 schools and received the trophy.

👩‍🏫

Best Teacher Awards

3 December (World Disability Day): Thiru M. Pradeep Kumar, IAS, District Collector of Trichy, presented the Best Teacher Award to our special educators Mr. Vincent and Mrs. Mary Yoga Latha.

🌟

25+ Years of Service Honours

28 September 2022: The Trichy Disability Forum honoured staff who served 25+ years in the field of disability — awards to Mrs. M. Lilli and Mrs. R. Kala from Thiru M. Pradeep Kumar, IAS. At the College Centenary, Tmt. Kanimozhi, MP, honoured caretaker Mrs. R. Kala for her generous service.

Life at Blossoms

Joyful days, all year round 🎉

🦋

Trips & Outings

One-day trip to the Butterfly Park and Kallanai with parents; a movie outing to watch Amaran (12 Nov 2024); staff trip to Hogenakkal (15 Feb 2025).

🩺

Medical Camp

Eye, general & dental care with Dr. Vinoth (Vinoth Eye Hospital), Dr. Praveen (Kauvery Hospital) and Dr. Sujatha (Thillai Dental Care) — nearly 75 people benefited.

🎓

Academic Milestones

Special coaching helps our adult learners complete their 10th standard exams — 7 students are writing the public exam this year, and 25 children learn inclusively in neighbourhood schools.

🎨

Concept Days

Monthly concept days — Red Colour Day, Fruits Day, Shapes, Community Helpers and fine & gross motor activity days — build social and communication skills.

👨‍👩‍👧

Special Parents' Sessions

“The Role of Self-Care and Building Resilience in Special Parents” (25 Jan 2025) — 50 parents benefited, along with monthly stress-free days.

💼

Vocational Training Day

Vocational Training Programme for Differently-Abled Women and their Parents (20 Dec 2024) — 30 beneficiaries.
