Admissions ✏️

We admit children between the ages of 0 and 22 years. Medium of instruction: English / Tamil. Every enquiry is answered with care — please reach out.

1️⃣

Step 1 — Enquire

Fill the form below or visit the school office at Holy Cross College, Trichy.

2️⃣

Step 2 — Assessment

Our multidisciplinary team assesses your child and understands their needs.

3️⃣

Step 3 — Blossom! 🌸

A need-based individual plan is made and your child's journey begins.

✅ Thank you! Your enquiry details are ready. Please tap the “Send by Email” button below (or note the details and contact the school office). We will be happy to help your child blossom. 🌸

🎒 Who can join?

  • Children aged 0–22 years
  • English / Tamil medium of instruction
  • Intellectual Disability, ASD, ADHD, Cerebral Palsy, Down Syndrome, Developmental Delay, Slow Learning, Speech & Learning Disorders, Multiple Disabilities
  • Government noon-meal scheme available
  • Mainstream (8th/10th) exam support & scribe facility where eligible
