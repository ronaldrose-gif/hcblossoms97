Understanding Intellectual Disability

What is Intellectual Disability?

Intellectual disability (intellectual developmental disorder) is characterised by significant limitations in intellectual functioning — the ability to learn, reason, solve problems and understand new information — and in adaptive behaviours such as self-care, communication and social interaction.

📘 Definition (AAIDD, 2021)

“Intellectual disability is a type of disability that includes many social and practical skills, is characterised by limitations in both mental functions and adaptive behaviours, and occurs before the age of 22 years.”

🔎 Characteristic features to watch for

  • Delay in head control / neck holding / sitting / crawling / standing / walking
  • Delay in language development; poor memory
  • Difficulty understanding social rules; poor problem-solving
  • Poor decision-making / logical thinking; inadequate attention
  • Indifference to surroundings, not responding; behavioural problems
  • Delay in learning tasks; scholastic backwardness
  • Associated conditions — blindness, deafness, cerebral palsy
  • Associated problems — epilepsy and hyperactivity
⚠️

Please act early

If one or more of these features are present in your child, professional help must be sought immediately. Early intervention makes a lifelong difference.

💡 Intervention

Intellectual Disability cannot be reverted by any treatment — but with professional training, most intellectually challenged persons can perform activities of daily living, become less dependent, and thereby enjoy an enhanced quality of life. That is exactly what we do at Blossoms.

Integrated Education

Learning together with everyone 🤝

  • Our special children are integrated into nearby Government-aided schools to improve social skills, behaviour patterns and to mingle with other children.
  • Training to write regular exams in the mainstream — especially 8th & 10th standard — for children with learning disability, mild intellectual disability and autism.
  • Children in special + integrated education receive individual care.
  • The school arranges scribe facility for special children to write Government exams.
  • At present, nearly 25 children pursue inclusive education in normal schools near their homes; 7 students are preparing for the 10th public exam with special coaching.
